Movement Module
================

This submodule of ``cartesiandomain`` contains the concrete ``Motion`` and
``TransportAcrossDomain`` implementations (see :doc:`../../superdrops/motion` and
:doc:`../../gridboxes/transport_across_domain`) used to move super-droplets through a
Cartesian domain and across its boundaries, including between MPI processes.
