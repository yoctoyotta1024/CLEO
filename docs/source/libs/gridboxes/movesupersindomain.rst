MoveSupersInDomain
====================

Header file: ``<libs/gridboxes/movesupersindomain.hpp>``
`[source] <https://github.com/yoctoyotta1024/CLEO/blob/main/libs/gridboxes/movesupersindomain.hpp>`_

Combines a ``Motion`` (:doc:`../superdrops/motion`) and a ``TransportAcrossDomain``
(:doc:`transport_across_domain`) to move every super-droplet in the domain for one timestep and
re-sort them into their new gridboxes (see :doc:`sortsupers`).
