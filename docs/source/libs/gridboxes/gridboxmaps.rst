GridboxMaps
=============

Header file: ``<libs/gridboxes/gridboxmaps.hpp>``
`[source] <https://github.com/yoctoyotta1024/CLEO/blob/main/libs/gridboxes/gridboxmaps.hpp>`_

The C++ concept providing the geometry of the domain (see the :ref:`glossary`):
converting between a gridbox's index and its physical coordinate bounds, and finding
neighbouring gridboxes. ``CartesianMaps`` (:doc:`../cartesiandomain/cartesianmaps`) is Cleo's
concrete implementation.
